# 25 — Content and Moderation Policy

Two parts: (A) the rules instructors and learners agree to (becomes `/legal/content-policy` after
legal review), and (B) how staff enforce them. Reviewers use part B during course review and
moderation.

## A. Rules (plain version)

### What you can't publish (courses, lessons, resources, posts, reviews)
1. Content you don't have the right to use: pirated courses, other people's videos, copyrighted books/PDFs, software cracks.
2. Illegal content or instructions for illegal activity (fraud, "yahoo" schemes, hacking accounts you don't own, fake documents, drug manufacture, weapons).
3. Get-rich-quick, guaranteed-income, ponzi/MLM recruitment, betting "sure odds", forex/crypto signals sold as guaranteed profit.
4. Medical, legal or financial claims presented as professional advice without qualification and disclaimer; anything encouraging self-harm or dangerous practices.
5. Hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, content sexualizing minors (zero tolerance, reported to authorities).
6. Exam leaks: real questions from professional or school exams (WAEC, JAMB, ICAN, etc.) presented as leaked or current papers. Past-question practice from legitimately published sources is fine.
7. Misleading certificates: claiming a Tokslearn certificate is accredited, licensed, or equivalent to a professional qualification unless proven and approved.
8. Off-platform sales: payment details, WhatsApp/Telegram links to sell the same content elsewhere, or asking learners to pay outside Tokslearn.
9. Spam: repetitive posts, affiliate link dumping, fake reviews, review swaps, reviewing your own course.
10. Personal data of others (phone numbers, addresses, screenshots of private chats).

### Quality minimums for courses
- At least 30 minutes of content (or 5 substantial lessons) for paid courses.
- Audible audio (no heavy background noise), readable screen recordings (≥ 720p), no long silent sections.
- Title and description accurately match content; outcomes are specific.
- Promotional material only in the final lesson or instructor bio (one link to own website allowed).
- Language of the course matches the language field.

### Consequences
Warning → content removed → strike → course unpublished → account suspended. Three strikes within 12
months = instructor privileges removed. Severe violations (child safety, fraud, piracy at scale)
skip steps. Earnings from removed pirated content are withheld and refunded to learners.

## B. Enforcement (staff playbook)

### Course review checklist (reviewer UI shows these as checkboxes)
1. Rights: no obvious copied content (spot-check 3 videos; reverse-search suspicious ones).
2. Rules 1–10 above: none violated.
3. Quality minimums met (sample start, middle, end of 3 lessons).
4. Accuracy: title/description/outcomes match; certificate type claims are correct.
5. Price reasonable for content (flag, don't reject, unless clearly deceptive).
6. Preview lessons representative.
7. Resources safe (open PDFs/zips in sandbox viewer; no executables).
Decisions: approve, request changes (notes required, reference checklist item), reject (policy violation, cite rule number). Target review time: 3 working days.

### Moderation queue
- Sources: user reports, automatic filters (links from new accounts, flagged words, many posts in short time), review anomalies (burst of 5-star reviews from new accounts).
- Priority: P0 child safety / threats (act within 1 hour, escalate), P1 fraud/illegal/piracy (24 h), P2 spam/harassment (48 h), P3 quality complaints (5 days).
- Actions: hide content, lock thread, warn user, strike, suspend, ban; each needs a rule number and note; all audited; user notified with the rule and appeal path (except spam bots).
- Appeals: one per action, handled by a different staff member.

### Piracy and takedowns
- Takedown requests via `legal@` or form: identify work, proof of ownership, URL. Remove within 48 h pending review; notify instructor with counter-notice option.
- Leaked Tokslearn content found elsewhere: watermark identifies the account; suspend and pursue.

### Refund abuse
- Pattern flags: > 3 refunds in 90 days, refunds right before thresholds on many courses, many accounts from same device/IP hash. Route to finance review; repeated abuse → refunds disabled for the account.
